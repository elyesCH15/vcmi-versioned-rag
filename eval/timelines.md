## Traps (stale or misleading text a retriever may surface)

- **T07**, 3.2 p14: "Companies are now able to make a Carbon Integrity Claim". Stale sentence left in Box 1 of v3.2; contradicts the cover and banners.
- **T10**, 1.0 p21: "within 24 months of obtaining a VCMI Claim". A different 24-month rule (long-term net-zero target), present v1-v2.1.
- **T18**, 3.2 p45: "The Steering Committee, co-chaired by Rachel Kyte and Tariye Gbadegesin". Acknowledgements in v3.2 still name the former co-chairs.

## Notes

- **T04**: v3.1 also dropped the 'whichever is the earlier' clause. Strong closed-book test: models trained before Aug 2025 will say 2026.
- **T05**: v2 to v2.1 swaps 'existing contractually committed carbon credits' for 'high quality credits': a subtle change worth one question.
- **T10**: Removed in v3.0 along with the SBTi-only requirement.
